Picking a weight: with 3–25kg adjustable dumbbells you've got room to grade each lift — heavier for compound presses/squats, lighter for isolation and raise work. Choose a weight per hand where the last 2 reps of a set are genuinely hard with good form.

Progression: once you hit the top of a rep range for all sets with good form, add 1–2.5kg per hand next session.

Rest between sets: 90s for big compound lifts (bench, row, squat, deadlift, overhead press), 45–60s for isolation moves (curls, raises, extensions, twists).
